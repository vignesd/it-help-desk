# Phishing and Suspicious Email

Phishing emails try to trick you into clicking a link, opening an attachment or sharing your
password. IT will **never** ask for your password by email, chat or phone.

## How to recognize a phishing email

- The sender address doesn't match the name, or uses a look-alike domain such as `examp1e.com`.
- The message creates urgency: "Your account will be closed in 24 hours", "Payment overdue".
- It asks you to sign in through a link, buy gift cards, or change bank details.
- Hovering over a link shows a different address than the text says.
- Outlook shows the banner **[EXTERNAL]** on a message that claims to come from a colleague.

## Report a suspicious email

1. Don't click any links or open attachments.
2. In Outlook, select the message and click **Report → Report phishing** on the ribbon.
   In Outlook on the web, use **... → Report → Report phishing**.
3. The message is moved out of your inbox and sent to the Security team automatically.
4. Don't forward the email to colleagues to ask whether it is real.

## I clicked a link or entered my password

Act immediately; the faster you act, the less damage an attacker can do.

1. Change your password right away at `password.example.com`.
2. Report the email with **Report phishing** as described above.
3. Contact the Service Desk and tell them you entered your password on a phishing page. This is
   treated as a security incident with top priority.
4. Watch for MFA push notifications you didn't request and **deny** them.

## I opened a suspicious attachment

1. Disconnect from the network: turn off Wi-Fi and unplug the network cable.
2. Don't shut down the computer; investigators may need its current state.
3. Contact the Service Desk by phone immediately and say you opened a suspicious attachment.

## A legitimate email went to Junk or was quarantined

1. In Outlook, open the **Junk Email** folder, select the message and choose **Report → Not junk**.
2. Quarantined messages are listed in the daily quarantine summary email. Click **Release** next to
   the message if you recognize the sender.
