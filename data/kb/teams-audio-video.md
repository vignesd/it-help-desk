# Microsoft Teams Audio and Video Problems

Microsoft Teams is the company tool for chat, calls and meetings. Most audio and video problems
come from Teams using the wrong device or from another app holding the camera or microphone.

## Others can't hear me in a Teams call

1. Check that you are not muted: the microphone icon in the meeting toolbar must not be crossed out.
2. In the meeting, click **More (...) → Settings → Device settings** and select the correct
   microphone. Headsets often appear as "Headset Microphone".
3. Unplug the headset, wait 10 seconds and plug it back in, then select it again.
4. Windows: open **Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone** and make sure
   **Let desktop apps access your microphone** is on.
5. macOS: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** and enable Microsoft Teams.

## I can't hear other people in a Teams call

1. Check the speaker selected under **More (...) → Settings → Device settings**.
2. Check the Windows volume mixer: right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar → **Volume mixer**
   and make sure Teams is not muted.
3. Make a test call: **Settings → Devices → Make a test call**. If the test call works, the problem
   is on the other person's side.

## Error "We couldn't find a microphone" or "We couldn't find a camera"

1. Close any other app that may use the device (Zoom, Webex, the Camera app, browser tabs with
   video calls).
2. Docking station users: plug the headset or webcam directly into the laptop instead of the dock.
3. Restart Teams completely: right-click the Teams icon in the system tray and choose **Quit**,
   then reopen it.
4. Restart your computer.
5. If the camera is still not detected, open Device Manager and check **Cameras** for a device
   with a yellow warning sign. Contact the Service Desk if one is shown.

## Camera shows a black screen

- Some laptops have a physical privacy shutter or a camera key (F8 or F10 with a camera icon).
  Open the shutter or press the key.
- In Teams, select the correct camera under **Device settings**. External webcams and the built-in
  camera are listed separately.

## Echo or poor call quality

- Use a headset instead of the laptop speakers to stop echo.
- Turn off your video if your connection is slow; audio needs far less bandwidth.
- On Wi-Fi, move closer to the access point or use a wired connection.
- Ask other participants to mute when they are not speaking.

## Teams is slow or keeps crashing

1. Quit Teams from the system tray.
2. Clear the Teams cache: press **Win + R**, enter `%appdata%\Microsoft\Teams`, and delete the
   contents of the folder (you will stay signed in on most devices; sign in again if asked).
3. Reopen Teams.
4. Update Teams: click **More (...) → Check for updates**.
