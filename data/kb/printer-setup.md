# Printer Setup and Common Errors

Office printers use **FollowMe printing**: you send a job to the single `FollowMe` queue and
release it at any printer by tapping your badge.

## Add the FollowMe printer

**Windows**
1. Connect to the office network or the VPN.
2. Open **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → Add device**.
3. Choose **Add manually**, then **Select a shared printer by name** and enter
   `\\print.corp.example.com\FollowMe`.

**macOS**
1. Open **Self Service** and run **Install FollowMe Printer**.

## Release a print job

1. Walk to any office printer.
2. Tap your badge on the reader.
3. Select your job and press **Print**. Jobs are deleted after 24 hours if not released.

## Common problems

**Job does not appear at the printer**
- Check that you printed to `FollowMe`, not a printer name left over from an old office.
- Wait one minute; large PDFs take time to spool.
- Make sure you are signed in to the laptop with your own account.

**Badge not recognised at the printer**
- Badges must be registered once: at the printer, tap the badge, then sign in with your
  username and password when prompted.

**Paper jam or out of toner**
- Follow the on-screen instructions on the printer. For toner, notify the office manager;
  replacement toner is stored in each print room.

**Printing is blank or garbled**
- Remove the FollowMe printer, restart the laptop, and add it again.
