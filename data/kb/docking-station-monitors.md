# Docking Station and External Monitors

Office desks have a USB-C docking station connected to two monitors, a keyboard, a mouse and a
wired network cable. One USB-C cable from the dock charges the laptop and connects everything else.

## External monitors show "No signal"

1. Check that the USB-C cable is plugged into the laptop port with the lightning or display icon.
   Other USB-C ports may not support video.
2. Press a key or move the mouse to wake the monitors.
3. Check the monitor input source: press the input button on the monitor and select
   **DisplayPort** or **HDMI**, depending on the cable used.
4. Unplug the dock's power cable, wait 30 seconds and plug it back in. Then reconnect the laptop.
5. Restart the laptop while it is connected to the dock.

## Monitors are detected but the layout is wrong

1. Right-click the desktop and choose **Display settings**.
2. Drag the numbered screens so they match how the monitors stand on your desk.
3. Under **Multiple displays**, choose **Extend these displays**.
4. Choose your main screen and check **Make this my main display**.

## Laptop is not charging on the dock

- Use the USB-C cable that came with the dock. Phone chargers and thin cables can't deliver enough
  power.
- Check that the dock's power light is on. If it is off, check the power cable and the outlet.
- If the laptop shows "Slow charger", the dock is too weak for your laptop model. Contact the
  Service Desk for a replacement dock.

## Keyboard, mouse or wired network doesn't work on the dock

1. Unplug the laptop from the dock and plug it back in.
2. Try a different USB port on the dock.
3. Update the dock firmware: open the Software Center and run the **Dock Firmware Update** package.
   Keep the laptop connected to the dock during the update.

## Screen flickers or the resolution is wrong

1. Open **Display settings** and set the resolution to the one marked **(Recommended)**.
2. Replace the monitor cable if the flicker continues; damaged cables are a common cause.
3. Install pending display driver updates from the Software Center.
4. If only one monitor flickers, swap the two monitor cables to find out whether the monitor or
   the cable is faulty, and report the faulty part to the Service Desk.
