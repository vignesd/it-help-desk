Setting Up Work Email on a Phone

Work email, calendar and Teams can be used on personal iPhone and Android phones. The phone must
be enrolled in Company Portal, which protects company data on the device. IT can only remove
company data and apps from enrolled personal phones, never your personal photos or files.

Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or Android 13 or later.
- A screen lock (PIN, fingerprint or face recognition).
- MFA set up with the Authenticator app.

Set up email on iPhone or Android

1. Install Company Portal from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open Company Portal and sign in with your company email and password.
3. Approve the MFA request and follow the steps to enroll the phone.
4. Install Microsoft Outlook from the App Store or Google Play.
5. Open Outlook and sign in with your company email.

The built-in Mail app on iPhone and Gmail on Android are not supported for work email.

Error "Your organization requires your device to be managed" or "Access blocked"

The phone is not enrolled or does not meet the requirements.

1. Open Company Portal and check the device status. Follow any steps it shows, for example
   setting a screen lock or updating the operating system.
2. Tap Check status to refresh it.
3. Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in to Outlook again.

Email stopped syncing on my phone

1. Check that the phone is online.
2. If you recently changed your password, sign out of Outlook on the phone and sign in again.
3. Open Company Portal and check that the device is still compliant.
4. Remove the account from Outlook and add it again.

Lost or stolen phone

1. Contact the Service Desk immediately so they can remove company data from the phone.
2. Change your password at password.example.com.
3. Remove the old phone from your MFA methods at mfa.example.com.
